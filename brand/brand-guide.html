Uinimate is a laboratory of motion: dependency-free CSS and SVG animations that work in any framework and can be tuned before you copy them. This guide is the reference for how the brand looks, moves and speaks.

Plate IConcept & voice

A laboratory of motion

Every animation is a specimen. It gets a catalogue number, it is measured, and its behaviour is plotted. Science, nature, geometry and astronomy frame the brand as annotations and textures. They suggest curiosity and learning, and never become the product itself.

Core values 1. Zero dependencies. 2. Works everywhere. 3. Make it yours. 4. Motion with manners. 5. Craft over quantity.
Voice Friendly, confident, short. A senior developer who enjoys the craft. "This one wiggles. Gently. You can make it wiggle harder."
Taglines Motion for any stack. No dependencies.
Copy. Tune. Ship. Anywhere.
Plate IILogo

The living i

The wordmark is set in upright Newsreader Medium. The dots on both i's are shapes that morph in turn: square, triangle, hexagon, circle, each with a spring. A hairline baseline with measuring ticks under each i borrows from the constructed wordmark. The logo is built in HTML and CSS, so the brand mark is itself a Uinimate specimen.

Plate IVColour

Paper, ink, blueprint and the ring

Light screens are paper. Dark screens are blueprint, never black. All colour comes from one formula, so twelve hues still read as one tone. OKLCH is the source of truth; hex is the fallback.

Base

Ring · oklch(0.83 0.10 h)

For shapes, fills, highlights and anything on blueprint. Text on the ring is always ink.

Ring deep · oklch(0.64 0.11 h)

For small marks on paper (logo dots, icon, chart lines) where the light ring would lack contrast. Not for text.

Plate VTypography

Newsreader, Space Mono, Inter

Newsreader carries the big words, Space Mono carries specs and labels, and Inter carries the docs. Newsreader is a variable font with weights from 200 to 800 and an optical-size axis, so boldness can be tuned at any time without changing the family. Big sizes use opsz 72 for tighter, sharper letters.

Plate IXMotion

How the brand moves

  • Shapes snap in with hard edges and settle with a spring. No blur, no glass, no fades longer than 240 ms.
  • Groups stagger by 40 ms. Big words move once; small labels keep moving around them.
  • Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion and has a designed still state.
  • Every easing is shown as a plotted curve in the docs.
